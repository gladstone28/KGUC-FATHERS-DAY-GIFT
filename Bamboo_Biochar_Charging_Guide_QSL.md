# Bamboo Biochar Charging Using Homemade Organic Nutrient Solutions

Prepared by Quality Solutions Limited (QSL)

Bamboo biochar can be successfully charged using homemade nutrient-rich liquids and organic materials including banana peels, green vegetables, eggshells, fish waste, animal manure, compost tea and worm castings.

## Suggested Formula
- 100 kg Bamboo Biochar
- 50 kg Goat Manure
- 25 kg Chicken Manure
- 20 kg Chopped Banana Peels
- 5 kg Crushed Eggshells

## Benefits
- Improved soil fertility
- Better water retention
- Enhanced microbial activity
- Increased crop productivity
