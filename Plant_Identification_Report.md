# Unidentified Gift Plant – Preliminary Identification Report

Plant Identification Assessment

• Sweet Pepper (Bell Pepper)
• Scotch Bonnet Pepper
• Habanero Pepper
• Other hot pepper varieties commonly grown in the Caribbean

• Smooth, slightly glossy leaves
• Oval to pointed leaf shape
• Alternate leaf arrangement on a green stem
• Overall appearance consistent with young pepper seedlings

Factors Creating Uncertainty:

• Transplant shock

Because the plant is still very young, other species can resemble it at this stage.

Alternative Possibilities:
• 70% probability – Pepper (Capsicum spp.)

2. Gently rub a leaf and check for a mild pepper-like scent.

4. Take additional photos when the plant reaches 15–20 cm (6–8 inches) tall.

Growing Advice:
The potting mix appears quite wet. Pepper plants generally prefer:
• Good drainage
• Watering only when the top inch of soil begins to dry
• Plenty of sunlight (6–8 hours daily)

Conclusion:
The plant is most likely a young pepper plant, but additional growth and photographs will be needed for a more confident identification.
